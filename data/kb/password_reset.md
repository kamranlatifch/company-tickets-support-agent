# Password reset

Customers can reset their password from the login page by clicking **Forgot password**.

Steps:
1. Go to app.cogentlabs.co/login
2. Click **Forgot password**
3. Enter your account email
4. Open the reset link within 15 minutes
5. Choose a new password (minimum 12 characters)

If the email does not arrive, check spam or contact support with your account email.
